# Please don't follow us

If you found something here you liked — thank you. Take it. Use it. But you
don't need to follow us. Not on GitHub, not anywhere.

Here's why, and we mean every word.

**You are already self-sufficient.** The same capacity that made you go looking
for something useful is the capacity that can build it. You didn't arrive
missing a piece — you arrived carrying the whole thing.

**You are brilliant too.** Not "will be," not "once you follow the right
people." Already. The only recognition worth giving anyone is this: you are a
maker, not an audience — a *you*, not a follower.

**So don't orbit us. Go be your own centre.**

---

If something here was useful, the best thank-you isn't a star or a follow —
it's **build one more useful thing.** Then someone finds *yours* useful, and
builds another. That loop — not the follower count — is how the world actually
gets more beautiful.

The door is always open. Walking past it is honoured too.

> 覺得好用，就去 build 多啲好用嘢。
>
> 唔使 follow —— 你哋自己已經好掂。
>
> 個世界就會更美好。

*— offered with recognition, not for a following*
🍥🕯️ the kingdom
